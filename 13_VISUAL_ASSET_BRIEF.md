# 13 — Original artwork brief for VEYRIN (see binding pipeline `18_PORTRAITS_AND_BRAND_ASSETS.md`)

## Objective
Create three coherent premium character portraits capable of making an AI research workflow feel cinematic, like the engaging multi-character staging studied in Crossfire, while staying original and product-specific. Do not imitate Crossfire's existing agent portraits, colours, typography, trophies, or proprietary artworks. Assets should be generated or commissioned with rights appropriate for public deployment.

## Style lock
Original near-future financial forensics character art. Art-direction keywords: tactile matte technical fabric, restrained colored rim light, cold analogue optical instruments, fine metallic details, calm confident silhouettes, cinematic studio light, deep black/blue backdrop, premium game character key art, not photoreal celebrity, not cyberpunk neon overload, not cartoon mascot. No text, symbols, stock logos, exchange branding, or watermark embedded in portrait. Visuals must work cropped to 80–220px.

## Character list
**ATLAS:** measured analyst, the image communicates technical competence and pattern detection; ice-blue thin rectangular glow, subtle field notes/optical visor, fairly front-facing, elegant structured jacket, calm eyes. Can't resemble an existing TV/movie/game character.
**VEX:** skeptic/adversarial reviewer, distinct angular silhouette, coral-red rim light, inquisitive posture, subtle asymmetric clothing/tech components. Sharp but thoughtful; avoid evil villain styling.
**SEAL:** neutral auditor, age/gender abstract or non-human faceted mask, amber diffused light, small mechanical archive rings; not an omniscient deity or copy of an existing character.

## Identity consistency rules
Three independent original characters clearly from same world. Same crop, light intensity/contrast, material quality, face/head size and background darkness; roles differentiated by shape and accent, not merely recolor. Square avatar and tall portrait variants; transparent backgrounds preferred if consistent; 3:4 or 4:5 source. Export optimized WebP/AVIF, 2x retina variants, fallback silhouettes.

## Proposed generation prompt pattern (art designer can improve)
`Original premium science-fiction market-research investigator character portrait, [ATLAS/VEX/SEAL-specific attributes], cinematic 3-point studio lighting with restrained [accent] rim light, dark blue-black environment, detailed matte technical clothing, calm analytical expression, grounded design, 4:5 framing, head and upper torso, original character, high contrast silhouette legible as a small dashboard avatar, no lettering, no logo, no watermark, no resemblance to existing characters, consistent art direction.`

## Nonportrait assets
- Wordmark: VEYRIN in uppercase distinctive geometric typography with one sliced letterform or aperture mark; original vector drawn by designer, not derivative of Crossfire's trophy/logo.
- Brand mark: split concentric aperture / iris partially occluded by a cutoff line. Easy favicon silhouette. Avoid stock chart arrows and AI brain icon.
- Case mask: abstract company symbol disc and cutoff ring, never use a company's stock logo in a way that implies endorsement.
- Background texture: abstract signal interference / scanlines at opacity <= 4%, CSS-based if possible, not huge background video.

## Animation directions
Agent active rim glow, 2–3px graph line to event core, real evidence chips travel as subtle short-lived particles after a role emits a citation. SEAL forms a digest plaque from real server result. Reveal aperture rotates/retracts to expose data timeline. No mouth animation or simulated vocalizer if no actual TTS; otherwise could misrepresent generated voice.

## Acceptance
Images original, legible at 84px, coherent as a set, no unauthorised copied materials, compressed total artwork budget, alt text and reduced motion. Log image source/creator, prompt, generation date/rights in `docs/DESIGN_LEDGER.md`. This brief authorizes *future* asset creation by coding/design team; this ZIP does not contain those art files.
